Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about SkillForge.

Is SkillForge free to use?
Yes, SkillForge is 100% free for students and aspiring professionals looking to build structured career paths and discover top learning resources.
Can I request a new career roadmap?
Absolutely! You can submit a request using our contact form above with your desired domain, and our curriculum team will review it for upcoming updates.
How often are resources updated?
Our resource library is continuously reviewed and updated on a monthly basis to ensure all links, tools, and tutorials remain relevant and high-quality.
Can I contribute resources?
We love community contributions! You can reach out via our contact form or visit our GitHub repository to submit pull requests and suggestions.
